# Match Day Nutrition Timeline

## Introduction

Match-day nutrition is about arriving with full fuel stores, staying properly hydrated, and avoiding anything that could cause digestive discomfort during play. This guide summarizes a practical timeline from the day before through post-match recovery.

## 24 Hours Before a Match

- Prioritize carbohydrate intake to maximize glycogen stores (~7-10 g/kg body weight for the day; see `football_nutrition.md`).
- Stick to familiar, well-tolerated foods — match day is not the time to try new dishes.
- Maintain steady hydration throughout the day rather than over-drinking at once.
- Get a full night's sleep; avoid late, heavy meals close to bedtime.

## 4 Hours Before a Match

- Eat a balanced pre-match meal: carbohydrate-focused, moderate protein, low fat and low fibre to ensure it digests comfortably.
- Example: rice or roti with dal, a small portion of vegetables, and curd.
- Avoid very spicy, fried, or heavy foods that can cause sluggishness or stomach upset.
- Continue hydrating steadily (see `hydration_guide.md` pre-training targets).

## 1 Hour Before a Match

- Keep intake light: a banana, a small carbohydrate snack, or a sports drink if tolerated.
- Avoid large volumes of food or fluid right before kickoff to prevent discomfort.
- A final small hydration top-up (200-300 ml) if urine is dark or the player feels thirsty.
- Some players benefit from a small caffeine intake (e.g., tea) if it is already part of their routine — match day is not the time to introduce it for the first time.

## During the Match

- Use half-time and any breaks to rehydrate; sip rather than gulp.
- In hot/humid conditions or when play extends (extra time), a carbohydrate-electrolyte drink can help sustain energy and fluid balance.
- Quick-access carbohydrate (a few sips of a sports drink, or an isotonic option) can help maintain blood glucose in the second half.

## After the Match

- Begin rehydration and a carbohydrate + protein recovery snack/meal as soon as practical (see `recovery_guide.md` for the full recovery checklist).
- Address any electrolyte losses, especially after matches in heat.
- Prioritize a full recovery meal within a few hours and prioritize sleep that night.

## Match Day Timeline Summary

| Time | Focus |
|---|---|
| 24 hours before | High carbohydrate intake, hydration, familiar foods, good sleep |
| 4 hours before | Balanced, low-fat/low-fibre meal with carbohydrate focus |
| 1 hour before | Light snack, small hydration top-up |
| During the match | Sip fluids at breaks; electrolytes/carbohydrate if hot or extended play |
| After the match | Rehydrate, recovery meal, electrolyte replacement, prioritize sleep |
